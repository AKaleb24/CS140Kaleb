Welcome to Gundam Noob

I created this website because Gundam is a hobby I love. I want to share that interest with my friends, classmates, and anyone curious about starting a build.

Why I like Gundam

I chose Gundam for this website because it is a hobby I genuinely enjoy. Researching it does not feel like just another assignment. It gives me an excuse to spend more time learning about something I already think is cool.

What interests me most is how different each model can look. I enjoy comparing designs and thinking about which one I would want to display. For me, choosing a kit is not only about finding the biggest or most expensive option. It is about finding a design that makes me excited to build.

I named this website Gundam Noob because I want it to feel welcoming. You do not need to know every mobile suit or understand every grade before becoming interested in the hobby. This website is a place where I can share what I learn in a way that makes sense to someone starting out.

My goal is to help my friends and classmates explore five grades: Entry Grade, High Grade, Real Grade, Master Grade, and Perfect Grade. Hopefully, something here catches your attention and makes you want to explore Gundam for yourself.

Compare the five grades

These approximate shopping ranges are in US dollars, not exact prices for every kit. They exclude shipping, taxes, tools, and optional accessories. They are guide estimates rather than live price quotes.

Difficulty and coolness are my personal ratings. Grades are not a strict skill ladder, and individual kits can offer different experiences. The skills column describes what I would practice, not official prerequisites.
