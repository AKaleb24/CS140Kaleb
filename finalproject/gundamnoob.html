Welcome to Gundam Noob

I created this website because Gundam is a hobby I love. I want to share that interest with anyone curious about starting a build.

Why is Gundam cool

What's interesting in Gundam is how different each model can look. There is soo much of it that you can compare designs and think about which one you would want to display. Choosing a kit is not only about finding the biggest or most expensive option. It is about finding a design that makes you excited to build.

My goal is to help any one that wants to get a base in this topic to explore five grades: Entry Grade, High Grade, Real Grade, Master Grade, and Perfect Grade. Hopefully, something here catches your attention and makes you want to start.

Compare the five grades

These approximate shopping ranges are in US dollars, not exact prices for every kit. They exclude shipping, taxes, tools, and optional accessories. They are guide estimates rather than live price quotes.

Difficulty and coolness are a personal ratings. Grades are not a strict skill ladder, and individual kits can offer different experiences. The skills column describes what I would practice, not official prerequisites.

My comparison of five Gundam model-kit grades
GradeApproximate price (USD)My difficulty ratingSkills I would practiceMy coolness rating

1 / 5: Very approachableFollowing diagrams, identifying parts, and checking how pieces connect.2 / 5: Look more cute than cool
High Grade (HG)$12–$35 for many standard kits
2 / 5: Beginner-friendlyUsing model nippers, checking part orientation, and carefully cleaning up leftover plastic.3 / 5: That's the beginning of coolness
Real Grade (RG)$25–$50
3.5 / 5: Detailed and preciseHandling small parts, organizing pieces, and following detailed assembly steps.4-5 / 5: Some are cooler than other
Master Grade (MG)$40–$90
4 / 5: A longer projectOrganizing a multi-stage build, assembling frame sections, and tracking parts.4-5 / 5: A lot of them are cool but it might depend on you style
Perfect Grade (PG)$150–$350; some new releases cost more
5 / 5: A major projectPlanning multiple sessions, organizing many parts, and checking complex assemblies.5 / 5: Perfection if you find the one for you

Source: LA Scale Model: Gunpla grades explained.

Oversized, limited-edition, and premium kits may cost more. My approach would be to choose a design I like and research that specific kit rather than buy every grade in order.
