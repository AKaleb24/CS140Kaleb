Entry Grade (EG)

Entry Grade is the starting point for someone who is curious about Gundam models but does not want a complicated first project. It introduces the enjoyment of assembly without making the hobby feel like a huge commitment.

What is Entry Grade?

Gundam kits in the Entry Grade line are generally 1/144 scale. They focus on straightforward assembly while still creating a poseable model. Kits such as the Entry Grade RX-78-2 Gundam are designed so the parts can be removed without nippers.

Source: PREX Hobby: Gunpla grade guide.

How to approach the build

Your goal with an Entry Grade kit would be to learn the process rather than rush through it. You could practice recognizing part numbers, understanding the diagrams, and checking the direction of each piece.

  • Read each step before connecting its parts.
  • Keep the workspace organized so loose pieces are easy to find.
  • Check the instructions again if something does not seem to fit.

These are personal advice rather than official requirements for this grade.
