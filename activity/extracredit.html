October 2026 General Conference Talks

Gambling

Speaker: President D. Todd Christofferson

President Christofferson taught that gambling is morally wrong. He warned that gambling can become addictive and harm people, families, money, school, work, and relationships.

(Note: it is not brand new information stop acting like it was lol)

Testimony

Speaker: Elder Gérald Caussé

Elder Caussé taught that a testimony of Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ is a gift. Testimony can grow through prayer, reading scriptures, obedience, and faith.

(My thought: it can also grow from sharing it)

Flying

Speaker: Elder Patrick Kearon

Elder Kearon used flying a glider as an example. Just as air currents lift a glider, Jesus Christ and the Holy Spirit can lift us when life is hard.
